Cadence

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Cadence ("we," "us," "the app") is a content scheduling tool operated by Velarox Solutions. This policy explains what information Cadence collects, why, and how it's handled.

1. What we collect

  • Account credentials for connected platforms. When you connect a social account (Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, LinkedIn, and others as supported), we store the access tokens or app passwords required to publish on your behalf. These are encrypted at rest.
  • Content you create in Cadence. Post captions, scheduled times, uploaded media (images and video), and categories/labels you assign.
  • Basic account info. If you create a Cadence login, we store your email and a hashed password.
  • Engagement metrics we can retrieve from connected platforms (e.g. follower counts, post performance) where a platform's API makes this available, so Cadence can show you analytics.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels. We do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

2. How we use it

  • To publish your scheduled posts to the platforms you've connected, at the times you've chosen.
  • To display your queue, calendar, and post history back to you inside Cadence.
  • To show engagement analytics for content you've posted through Cadence.
  • To operate core app functionality (authentication, media storage, notifications about failed publishes).

We do not use your content or credentials for any purpose other than operating the app on your behalf.

3. How it's stored

Cadence runs on infrastructure we operate directly. Platform credentials (OAuth tokens, app passwords) are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption. Media you upload is stored on the same infrastructure and is not publicly accessible except when published to a platform you've explicitly connected and authorized.

4. Third-party platforms

When you connect a social platform to Cadence, that platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle data on their end. Cadence only requests the minimum permissions needed to schedule and publish posts (and, where available, read engagement metrics) — we do not request access to direct messages, contacts, or other data unrelated to posting.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect any connected account at any time from the Accounts screen, which removes the stored credential immediately. You can delete individual posts and media from your library at any time. If you'd like your full account and all associated data deleted, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

Cadence is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at privacy@velaroxsolutions.com.
